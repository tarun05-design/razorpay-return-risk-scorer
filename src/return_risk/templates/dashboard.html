Olist has no native "returned" column. Rather than faking labels, we formulated an honest proxy verified with natural language review audits:

  • Label Formula: canceled status OR delivered with review score ∈ {1, 2}.
  • Dropped Neutral: 3-star reviews and unreviewed orders are excluded to avoid label poisoning.
  • Portuguese Review Audit: Low-score reviews mention return/refund terms (devolver, estorno, cancelamento, reembolso) in 20.8% of cases vs. 0.36% for high scores.
  • 57× Enrichment Ratio: Proves the proxy captures real return/refund sentiment, not generic noise.

How this project enforces strict production integrity:

  • Expanding Seller Prior: Sellers are scored using strictly prior timestamped orders with empirical Bayes shrinkage. Zero future leakage.
  • Held-Out Time Split: Last 20% of orders by purchase date (May 29, 2018 cutoff) held out forward in time.
  • Strictly Defense-Only: Protects merchants from loss; zero adversarial or offensive capability.
  • Tree Boosting over LLMs: HistGradientBoostingClassifier delivers sub-millisecond inference (<1ms) and perfectly calibrated probabilities.
